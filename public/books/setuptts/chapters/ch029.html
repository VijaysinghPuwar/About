Style and integrity checks on the finished book

A second, independent pass then checked the manuscript claim by claim against the source: about 260 concrete claims (constants, line references, names, behaviours, CI and packaging details, diagram labels). It found 22 discrepancies, from a wrong back-off formula and a wrong release attribution to imprecise wording, and every one was corrected before the final build. It also surfaced the dead dash-collapsing rule described in Chapter 6.

The generated text was searched for em dashes (none), for filler phrases, for duplicated headings, and for anything resembling a key, token or password (none; the edge-tts client token is deliberately not reproduced). Every YouTube URL was re-resolved after the book was built. Code excerpts were compared with the source at the analysed commit.
