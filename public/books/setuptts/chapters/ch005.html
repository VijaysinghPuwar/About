3.1 How the directories relate

The dependency direction is strict and worth remembering:

ui  ──>  workers  ──>  services  ──>  edge-tts / aiohttp
 │          │             │
 └──────────┴─────>  utils, models, config  (no Qt widgets, no network)
  • app/ui knows about workers and services but never talks to the network or writes staging files itself. OutputPanel is the controller: it reads the form, validates it and submits jobs.
  • app/workers contains everything that runs on a background thread, plus JobQueue, which lives on the UI thread and owns the workers. tts_worker.py and chunk_store.py together are the reliability core, about 4,100 lines.
  • app/services wraps external systems: the speech service and the SQLite database, plus the pure-Python text analysis in tts_quality.py.
  • app/utils, app/models, app/config are small, mostly pure helpers. Several were written specifically to move a failure from “hours into a job” to “before the job starts”: output_paths.py is the clearest example.
